Speculative Decoding

Why AI writes a secret first draft.

A smaller model drafts candidate tokens. The main model scores them together, and the algorithm accepts candidates in order until the first rejection. The guide follows the bridge-at-dawn example from the Reel.

The midnight check: If the draft assigns 50% and the main model assigns 30% to the proposed token in the same context, its acceptance chance is min(1, 0.30 ÷ 0.50) = 60%.
