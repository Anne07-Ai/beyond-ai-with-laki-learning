What is an LLM?

How ChatGPT generates answers, explained without the jargon.

One-minute takeaway: A Large Language Model is an AI system trained on large collections of data to learn patterns in language. It generates text by predicting and selecting successive tokens based on context.

1. Think of a familiar phrase

If someone says, “Twinkle, twinkle, little…”, you may expect the word “star.” You recognise a familiar pattern. This is a simplified analogy for next-token prediction — not a full explanation of how an LLM works.

2. What does “LLM” mean?

Large Language Model describes a type of AI model trained to process and generate language. During training, it learns statistical patterns and relationships from data, rather than being given a fixed list of answers to every question.

3. How does ChatGPT respond?

Imagine asking: “Why is the sky blue?” The model processes your prompt as tokens, uses the context of the conversation and generates an output token, then another, until it forms a response.

Question→Tokens→Context→Next token→Repeat

This repeated process is often called autoregressive generation. The model's next-token distribution depends on learned parameters and the tokens already in context. This does not mean each answer is a direct copy of a stored page.

4. What is a token?

A token is a unit of text the model processes. Depending on the tokenizer, it may represent a complete word, a word fragment, punctuation or another character sequence.

Example: A longer word might be represented by multiple tokens, but the exact split varies by model. We will explore this in Day 2.

5. Can an LLM make mistakes?

Yes. An LLM can produce a fluent, confident-sounding statement that is inaccurate or invented. This is often called an AI hallucination. A polished answer is not proof that a claim is true.

Safety tip: Check important facts against trusted sources, especially for health, finance, legal matters and cybersecurity.

Quick quiz

  1. Does an LLM simply copy every answer from a book?
    Answer: No. It uses learned patterns to generate responses.
  2. Does one token always equal one word?
    Answer: No. Tokens can be words, parts of words or punctuation.
  3. Can an AI answer sound confident and still be wrong?
    Answer: Yes. Verify important information.

Key words

LLM: Large Language Model. Token: A unit processed by a language model. Context: The information in the prompt and available conversation. Hallucination: An AI-generated claim that is unsupported or false.
